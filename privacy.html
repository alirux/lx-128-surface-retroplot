NAME

privacy - what LIRUX 3D does with your data

SYNOPSIS

No cookies. No accounts. No analytics. The formulas you type are computed in your browser and never leave it. Everything the page needs, fonts included, comes from this site: your browser contacts no one else.

DATA CONTROLLER

Alberto Lirussi. For anything about this notice, or to exercise your rights, write to privacy@codingful.com.

WHAT YOU TYPE

Formulas, commands and the names you type at the login: prompt are handled only by the page, on your device. None of them is sent to a server or saved.

The last formula is written in the page address, after the # sign, so you can share a plot by copying the link. Browsers never send that part of the address to the server.

SAVE creates the PNG image on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

LOCAL STORAGE

The page sets no cookies. It keeps two values in your browser's local storage, to start again on the computer you used last:

lirux3d.machine
the last computer used: 128, 64 or the terminal
lirux3d.home
the computer the terminal was dialled from, to go back to it on logout

They contain no identifier, stay in your browser, are sent to no one, and you can delete them at any time from your browser settings. They are technical preferences needed to give you the page the way you left it, so they require no consent.

SERVER LOGS

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure automatically records technical data with each request: IP address, browser and operating system, date and time, page requested and referring page. This data serves to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running a working and safe service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub Pages does not give the controller access to these logs: the controller never reads, stores or processes them. They remain with GitHub under its own privacy statement.

TRANSFERS OUTSIDE THE EU

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States and may process browsing data outside the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR, namely the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission and the participation of the Microsoft group in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

PROFILING

There is no tracking, no profiling and no automated decision about you.

EXTERNAL LINKS

The page links to GitHub and LinkedIn. Following those links you leave this site and enter services with their own privacy notices, over which the controller has no control.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to privacy@codingful.com. As the controller keeps no data about you, the answer will often be that there is none.

If you think your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

CHANGES

Updates to this notice are published on this page, with a new revision date below.
